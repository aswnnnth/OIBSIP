# DATA ANALYTICS – LEVEL 1 – RETAIL SALES

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship (OIBSIP)**.

The project focuses on performing **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a retail sales dataset to understand sales performance, identify trends, compare product performance, and generate meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify the highest-performing regions
- Compare sales across product categories
- Analyze sales by sub-category
- Identify the highest-performing customer segment
- Compare profit across product categories
- Generate business insights and recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Overall Sales Performance
Calculated the total sales generated during the period covered by the dataset.

**Total Sales:** Approximately **$2.30 million**

### 2. Sales by Region
Analyzed sales across different regions.

**Key Insight:**  
The **West region** generated the highest total sales.

### 3. Sales by Product Category
Compared sales across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.

**Key Insight:**  
**Technology** generated the highest total sales.

### 4. Sales by Sub-Category
Analyzed sales performance across different product sub-categories.

**Key Insight:**  
**Phones** generated the highest total sales, followed closely by Chairs.

### 5. Sales by Customer Segment
Compared sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

**Key Insight:**  
The **Consumer segment** generated the highest sales.

### 6. Profit by Category
Analyzed total profit generated by each product category.

**Key Insight:**  
**Technology** generated the highest overall profit, while Furniture generated the lowest profit.

## 💡 Business Insights

- Total sales were approximately **$2.30 million**.
- The **West region** was the strongest-performing region in terms of sales.
- **Technology** was the highest-selling and most profitable category.
- **Phones** were the top-performing sub-category by sales.
- The **Consumer segment** contributed the highest revenue.
- Furniture had relatively good sales but generated the lowest profit, indicating potential margin or cost-related issues.

## 📌 Recommendations

- Continue investing in high-performing Technology products.
- Maintain strong inventory levels for top-selling products such as Phones and Chairs.
- Develop targeted marketing strategies for lower-performing regions and segments.
- Review pricing, discounts, and costs in the Furniture category to improve profitability.
- Use targeted promotions to improve sales in lower-performing product sub-categories.
